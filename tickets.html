Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How will I receive my tickets?

An electronic ticket with a QR code will be sent to your email immediately after booking confirmation.

How early should I arrive before the match?

Gates at the Santiago Bernabéu Stadium open 2 hours prior to kickoff.

Do I need an ID card for entry?

Yes, a valid personal identification document matching the name on the ticket must be presented at the gate.
